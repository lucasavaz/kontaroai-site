Account deletion

Delete your Kontaro account

You can delete your Kontaro account (app name “Kontaro”, by Kontaro Inc.) at any time, yourself, in the app. You don’t need to contact us.

In the app (iPhone, Android) or the web app

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Privacy & legal.
  3. Tap Delete my account.
  4. Enter your password.
  5. Confirm.

Without the app

Send an email to talk@kontaro.ai from the email address of your Kontaro account, with the subject “Delete my account”. We’ll confirm your request by email and delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted

If you are a team member

Your sign-in, your sessions and the phones registered to your account are deleted. The conversations you handled belong to your company and stay in its workspace.

If you are the only owner of the workspace

The entire workspace is deleted: conversations, contacts, campaigns, AI agents, data and all team accounts. Connected channels (such as WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram) are disconnected and the card subscription is cancelled. The data is erased within minutes. If you want to keep a copy, export it before you delete the account.

What may be kept

  • Invoices and payment records that the law requires us to keep (in Stripe and in our accounting records), for up to 5 years.
  • Backups, which expire and are erased automatically within 30 days.

Nothing else is kept. For more details, read our Privacy Policy. Questions: talk@kontaro.ai.
