1. Summary

  • We collect what we need to run Kontaro: your account details, billing details and the messages and contacts you manage in the app.
  • For your customers’ messages, we act on your behalf (as your processor). You decide what is collected and why.
  • We do not sell personal data, and we don’t use your customers’ messages to train AI models for other customers.
  • Our mobile apps have no advertising, no tracking across apps and no analytics SDKs.
  • You can access, export or delete your data, and you can delete your account yourself in the app. Contact talk@kontaro.ai.

2. Who is responsible

Kontaro Inc. (830 Brickell Plaza, Miami, FL 33131, United States) is the controller for personal data about website visitors and account users (our customers and their team members).

For personal data about our customers’ own customers, such as their messages, names, phone numbers and Instagram or Telegram IDs, the business that uses Kontaro is the controller and Kontaro is the processor (“operador” under Brazil’s LGPD; “processor” under the EU/UK GDPR). We process that data only on the business’s instructions and to provide the Service to it.

3. Data we collect

  • Account data: name, email, company, phone, password (stored hashed), language and settings.
  • Billing data: plan, invoices and payment status. Card details are handled by our payment processor (Stripe) and are not stored by us.
  • Customer Data you manage in Kontaro: contacts, messages and media from WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram, campaign lists, files and spreadsheets you connect (for example from Google Drive), and AI configuration.
  • Mobile app data: a push notification token and the files you choose to send. See Mobile apps.
  • Usage and technical data: logs, device and browser information and IP address, used for security and to keep the Service working.
  • Website: if you fill in the sign-up form, your name and email. The website stores your language choice in your browser. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies on this website.

4. How we use it

  • To provide, maintain and secure the Service, including sending and receiving messages, delivering notifications and generating AI replies and insights.
  • To create and manage your account, verify your email, bill you and provide support.
  • To send service notices, such as billing, security and changes to our terms. Marketing emails only with your consent, and you can unsubscribe anytime.
  • To comply with law and enforce our terms.

Legal bases (where GDPR or LGPD apply): performance of a contract, legitimate interests (security, service improvement), consent (marketing) and legal obligations.

5. Mobile apps

The Kontaro apps for iOS and Android work with the same account as the web app. In addition to the data above, they handle:

  • Push notification token: a technical identifier for your device, used only to deliver notifications about new messages and conversations. Notifications are delivered through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service. You can turn notifications off in the app (Settings → This device) or in your phone’s settings.
  • Photos, files and voice notes: only when you choose to send them in a conversation. The app asks for permission before it uses your camera, photo library or microphone, and you can change these permissions at any time in your phone’s settings.
  • Face ID / fingerprint lock: if you turn on the app lock, the check is done by your phone’s operating system. Kontaro never receives your biometric data; the app only stores, on your device, whether the lock is on or off.

The apps contain no advertising, do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, do not include analytics SDKs, and we do not sell your data.

6. Service providers and AI

We share data with providers that help us run the Service, under contracts that protect it. They include:

  • Meta (WhatsApp and Instagram messaging);
  • Telegram (only when a business connects a Telegram bot);
  • cloud hosting (for example Render);
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service (push notifications in the mobile apps);
  • AI model providers that generate AI replies: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and xAI, or the AI provider a business connects with its own account;
  • Stripe (payments);
  • email delivery providers;
  • Google (only if you connect Google Drive).

AI providers process message content only to generate responses for you and, under our agreements, do not use it to train their models. When a business connects its own AI provider account, that provider’s terms with the business also apply. A current list of subprocessors is available on request.

7. International transfers

Kontaro is based in the United States and our providers may process data in the US and other countries. When we transfer personal data from Brazil, the EU/UK or other regions, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses, as required by applicable law.

8. Retention

We keep account and Customer Data while your account is active. After you cancel, we delete or anonymize Customer Data within 90 days, except where we must keep certain records (for example invoices) by law. You can export your data before cancelling. If you delete your account, section 9 applies.

9. Deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself in the Kontaro app (iOS or Android) or the web app: Settings → Privacy & legal → Delete my account, enter your password and confirm. Without the app, email talk@kontaro.ai from your account’s email address with the subject “Delete my account”; we confirm and delete it within 30 days.

  • For a team member, we delete their sign-in, sessions and registered phones. Conversations they handled stay with their company.
  • For the only owner of a workspace, we delete the entire workspace: conversations, contacts, campaigns, AI agents, data and all team accounts. Connected channels are disconnected and the card subscription is cancelled. Data is erased within minutes.
  • We may keep invoices and payment records required by law (in Stripe and our accounting records, for up to 5 years) and backups, which expire within 30 days.

Full details: Delete your Kontaro account.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to certain uses, and to withdraw consent. This includes the rights under Brazil’s LGPD (Lei 13.709/2018), the EU/UK GDPR and US state privacy laws such as California’s. To exercise them, email talk@kontaro.ai. If your data was collected by one of our customers (for example, you messaged a business that uses Kontaro), please contact that business; we will help them respond. You may also complain to your data protection authority, such as the ANPD in Brazil.

11. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls and monitoring to protect data. No system is completely secure, and we will notify affected customers and authorities of a personal data breach as required by law.

12. Children

Kontaro is for businesses and is not intended for people under 18. You must be at least 18 to create an account or use the apps. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children; if you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes and contact

We will post updates to this policy here and, for material changes, notify account owners by email. Contact: Kontaro Inc., 830 Brickell Plaza, Miami, FL 33131, United States · talk@kontaro.ai. Help with the apps: Support.
